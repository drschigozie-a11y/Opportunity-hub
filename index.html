How to use Opportunity Hub

Simple steps for a safer search.
🔎 Find a match

Search by opportunity type, study level, job level or keywords.

✅ Check the source

Read eligibility and deadline details, then confirm everything on the official site.

🔗 Apply directly

Use the official application link. Opportunity Hub does not ask you to pay to access listings.

We aim to check every listing, but users should always confirm details with the original organisation. Never send money to a recruiter promising a guaranteed job or scholarship.

Suggest an opportunity / Listing manager

For visitors: use this form to suggest a listing for review. Suggestions are not published automatically. After you deploy this site to Netlify, submissions can be configured through Netlify Forms if form detection is enabled for the site.

Owner's publishing checklist

Before adding a listing to the public catalogue: open the official organisation page, confirm the opportunity still exists, check who can apply, confirm pay/funding and deadline, and record the date you checked it. Never publish an unverified social-media repost as the only source.
